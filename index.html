“Wrong delivery is the executioner of a true word.”

Saadi Shirazi — Gulistan (Chapter VIII)

The truth of a statement does not depend on its tone; yet its ability to reach another human mind almost always does. The speaker is responsible for the grace of the delivery, and the listener for separating the essence from the vessel.
